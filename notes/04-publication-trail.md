# Publication trail — how the work developed

The publications follow the same path as the experiments: from the first glass–graphite studies and porous-glass passivation, through controlled surface relief and micro-optics, to a closer look at the microplasma itself and the stability of the processing conditions.

Many of these papers are also available through my [ResearchGate profile](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Roman-Zakoldaev).
